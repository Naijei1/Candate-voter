# Phased plan

Kentucky federal general election is the pilot. Later phases wait on a source.

1. Done in the app: ZIP or map to a House district, Senate plus that district, nominee cards, sourced issue notes, official portraits where a Clerk photo exists, compare tabs, finance totals kept separate from outside spending, Kentucky Lantern headlines, results links after 6 p.m. Eastern, email sign-in, and follows stored without email delivery.
2. Next, only with a source: campaign sites still missing, endorsements, more than the stored House votes, and a certified ballot question.
3. After that: state and local contests once a ZIP or address can be tied to those districts without storing the street.
4. Polls, speech analysis, and alert email stay off until a vendor, a transcript archive, and SES exist.

`REVAMP_PLAN.md` keeps the product notes. This file is the order of work.
